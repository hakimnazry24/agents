# ISO 10993-1:2025 — Biological evaluation (key BC)

**In plain words:** materials that touch a patient's body must be safe: no irritation, allergy or toxicity. The evaluation depends on what touches the body, where, and for how long.

**Relation to INNUTRIRE:** INNUTRIRE is software. The tablet is touched by staff, not placed on the patient. The parts that touch patients (calorimeter mask, canopy, ventilator circuit) belong to the calorimeter makers. The checks confirm that this stays true and that the statement is recorded.

---

### BC-01 Non-applicability statement recorded
- **Applies to:** all (normally kept once, outside or linked)
- **Clause:** scope (1)
- **Look for:** a statement such as "INNUTRIRE has no patient-contacting materials".
- **Met:** present. **Gap:** none. **Outside repo** if kept in the technical file.
- **Severity if not met:** Low

### BC-02 No patient-contacting accessory specified
- **Applies to:** provisioning, infrastructure, tablet-app
- **Clause:** 5 (categorisation by contact)
- **Look for:** docs or setup scripts that specify mounting the tablet on the patient or bed in contact with the patient, or supplying INNUTRIRE-branded sensors, straps or covers.
- **Met:** none (out of scope). **Gap:** a patient-contacting part is specified with no evaluation.
- **Severity if not met:** Medium

### BC-03 Cleaning and disinfection of shared tablets
- **Applies to:** provisioning, tablet-app
- **Clause:** observation only (cleaning agents can affect materials; infection control is a hospital procedure)
- **Look for:** guidance on cleaning tablets used at the ICU bedside, or a case and cover that can be disinfected.
- **Met:** documented. **Outside repo** otherwise; ask the team.
- **Severity if not met:** Low
