# ISO 14155:2026 — Clinical investigation of medical devices (key CI)

**In plain words:** how to run a study of a medical device on patients safely and properly: a written plan, ethics approval, informed consent, monitoring of data quality, reporting of problems, and a final report.

**Relation to INNUTRIRE:** the field study at 4 hospitals (target 300 patients) compares AI predictions with indirect calorimetry. Most evidence lives outside the code, so many items here are `Outside repo`. The repo checks focus on data integrity and on change control during the study.

No clause numbers are given here, because the 2026 edition's numbering was not confirmed from its preview.

---

### CI-01 Study protocol and endpoints referenced
- **Applies to:** all
- **Look for:** a reference to the clinical investigation plan, its endpoint (for example agreement between predicted and measured REE) and the statistical plan, including the holdout set.
- **Met:** referenced. **Outside repo:** not in the repo; ask for it.
- **Severity if not met:** High

### CI-02 Ethics approval, consent and registration
- **Applies to:** all
- **Look for:** references to ethics approval per site, consent handling and study registration (NMRR or ClinicalTrials.gov). The backend's consent field counts as a technical control only.
- **Met:** referenced. **Outside repo** otherwise.
- **Severity if not met:** High

### CI-03 Study data integrity
- **Applies to:** backend, tablet-app, monitoring
- **Look for:**
  - soft delete instead of hard delete;
  - timestamps on records;
  - an audit trail of edits (ties to CS-07);
  - validation of the link between a prediction and its gold standard;
  - detection of orphan or out-of-range records;
  - offline-sync conflict handling.
- **Met:** all of these. **Partial:** some. **Gap:** records can be changed or lost without a trace.
- **Severity if not met:** High

### CI-04 Source data and paper records reconciled
- **Applies to:** backend, monitoring
- **Look for:** a way to record or reconcile data collected on paper outside the app, such as an import tool or a field marking the data source.
- **Met:** exists. **Gap:** none. **Outside repo** if this is handled by a manual procedure.
- **Severity if not met:** Medium

### CI-05 Device version known for every study record
- **Applies to:** backend, tablet-app, monitoring
- **Look for:** whether each prediction stores the app version, backend version and AI model version used.
- **Met:** all three stored. **Partial:** some. **Gap:** none.
- **Severity if not met:** High

### CI-06 Changes during the study are controlled
- **Applies to:** backend, tablet-app
- **Look for:** a rule or record that changes to the device during the study (model swaps, calculation changes) are documented and assessed for impact on the study: changelog entries, release notes or a model-change log.
- **Met:** documented with impact notes. **Partial:** changelog only. **Gap:** none.
- **Severity if not met:** Medium

### CI-07 Adverse events and device problems can be reported
- **Applies to:** tablet-app, backend, monitoring
- **Look for:** an in-app or documented route to report device problems or adverse events, and error capture that reaches the team.
- **Met:** exists. **Partial:** technical error capture only. **Outside repo** for a clinical reporting procedure.
- **Severity if not met:** Medium

### CI-08 Training and holdout data separated and frozen
- **Applies to:** backend (it ships the deployed model and its training data in `innutrire/model/` and `innutrire/data/`)
- **Look for:** next to the deployed model: a documented, fixed holdout set; a check for patient overlap between the training and test sets; dataset version identifiers.
- **Met:** all of these. **Partial:** a holdout set but not frozen or versioned. **Gap:** none.
- **Severity if not met:** High
