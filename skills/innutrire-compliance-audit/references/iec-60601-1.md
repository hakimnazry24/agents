# IEC 60601-1 ed. 3.2 — Basic safety and essential performance (key ES)

**In plain words:** medical electrical equipment must not shock, burn or otherwise harm anyone, and must keep doing its essential job.

**Relation to INNUTRIRE:** INNUTRIRE is software on off-the-shelf tablets (Microsoft Surface) and ordinary servers, which fall under IT safety rules (IEC 62368-1), not this standard. It becomes relevant only if INNUTRIRE ships its own medical hardware or is electrically connected to medical equipment such as a calorimeter or ventilator (a "medical electrical system", clause 16). The checks here look for those triggers and for the justification that the standard does not apply.

---

### ES-01 Non-applicability justification recorded
- **Applies to:** all (normally kept once, outside or linked)
- **Clause:** scope (1)
- **Look for:** a statement that INNUTRIRE is software on general IT equipment, naming the tablet and server models and their safety approvals.
- **Met:** present. **Gap:** none. **Outside repo** if kept in the technical file.
- **Severity if not met:** Low

### ES-02 No cable link to medical electrical equipment
- **Applies to:** backend, tablet-app, provisioning, infrastructure
- **Clause:** 16 (ME systems)
- **Look for:** code or config that talks to a calorimeter, ventilator or monitor over serial, USB, RS-232 or a direct LAN link (`pyserial`, `serial.Serial`, `COM` ports, HL7 or device drivers, vendor SDKs).
- **Met:** no such link (the standard stays out of scope). **Gap:** a link exists but has no ME-system assessment.
- **Severity if not met:** High

### ES-03 No INNUTRIRE-specific hardware
- **Applies to:** provisioning, infrastructure
- **Clause:** scope (1)
- **Look for:** custom hardware, enclosures, power supplies or mounts specified as part of INNUTRIRE, in docs or provisioning scripts.
- **Met:** none (out of scope). **Gap:** custom hardware with no assessment.
- **Severity if not met:** Medium

### ES-04 Software in the essential performance chain
- **Applies to:** backend, tablet-app
- **Clause:** 14 (programmable electrical medical systems)
- **Look for:** whether INNUTRIRE output is fed automatically into any medical electrical equipment (for example setting a feeding pump).
- **Met:** output goes only to clinicians (out of scope). **Gap:** automatic control of equipment.
- **Severity if not met:** High
