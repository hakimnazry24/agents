# IEC 60601-1-2 ed. 4.1 — Electromagnetic compatibility (key EM)

**In plain words:** medical electrical equipment must not disturb other devices, and must not be disturbed by them, through radio waves or electrical noise.

**Relation to INNUTRIRE:** like IEC 60601-1, this applies to medical electrical hardware. INNUTRIRE's tablets and servers are general IT and radio equipment with their own EMC and radio approvals (CISPR 32/35; MCMC type approval for Wi-Fi in Malaysia). The checks look for triggers and for the recorded justification.

---

### EM-01 Non-applicability justification and equipment approvals recorded
- **Applies to:** all (normally kept once, outside or linked)
- **Clause:** scope (1)
- **Look for:** a statement naming the tablet and server models and their EMC and radio approvals.
- **Met:** present. **Gap:** none. **Outside repo** if kept in the technical file.
- **Severity if not met:** Low

### EM-02 Radio use near patients is documented
- **Applies to:** provisioning, tablet-app
- **Clause:** scope; wireless coexistence
- **Look for:** Wi-Fi and Bluetooth configuration in the provisioning scripts (`02-configure-wifi.ps1`), any Bluetooth or NFC use in the app, and guidance on using the tablet next to ICU equipment.
- **Met:** radios limited to hospital Wi-Fi, with documented guidance. **Partial:** radios configured but no guidance. **Gap:** extra radios enabled without reason.
- **Severity if not met:** Low

### EM-03 No wired link to medical equipment
- **Applies to:** backend, tablet-app, provisioning
- **Clause:** scope (ME systems)
- **Look for:** same signals as ES-02 (serial, USB or direct links to medical devices).
- **Met:** none (out of scope). **Gap:** a link exists with no EMC assessment.
- **Severity if not met:** Medium
